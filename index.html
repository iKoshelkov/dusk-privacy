DUSK – Idle Dimmer: Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026 · Publisher: The Wrong Button

The short version

DUSK doesn't collect, store, send or sell any personal data. It has no account, no ads, no analytics and makes no internet connections.

What DUSK does on your PC

To know when you've stepped away and when you're back, DUSK reads Windows' idle time and listens to keyboard and mouse activity locally. It only notes that input happened and whether it came from a physical device. It never records which keys you press or what you type, and nothing leaves your computer.

While "forced mode" is on, DUSK watches for the Esc key so that holding it can wake the screens.

What DUSK stores

Your settings are saved in a small text file on your PC (%APPDATA%\DUSK\settings.ini). If the app hits an error, technical details may be written to %APPDATA%\DUSK\error.log on your PC. Neither file is ever sent anywhere. Uninstalling DUSK removes them (Microsoft Store version).

Links

If you click "Support DUSK", your web browser opens the publisher's support page. That site has its own privacy policy.

Children

DUSK collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. Questions or support: open an issue (a free GitHub account is needed).
